Q6 (a) [2]

A trolley of mass 2.0 kg accelerates from rest to 6.0 m/s in 3.0 s. Calculate the resultant force on it.

a = (v − u) / t = 6.0 / 3.0 = 2.0 m/s²
F = ma = 2.0 × 2.0 = 4.0 N
